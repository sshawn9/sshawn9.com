> This is the complete second edition. New information has been placed where it reads naturally.

## Background

This sample article explains a subject that needs maintenance over a long lifetime.

## Scope

The approach suits long-lived articles that need ongoing additions and corrections. One-off essays normally do not need version directories.

## Current approach

The article now has two steps:

1. Record the current information.
2. Publish a new complete edition when the substance changes.

## Conclusion

Readers receive a coherent v2 instead of a series of patches appended to v1.
